Install this app on your iPhone
  1. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow pointing up) in Safari.
  2. Select Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

Allow camera use?

This app needs access to your camera only to show a local preview on screen.

The rear camera will be turned on. No image is sent to external servers: the video stays on your device.

Your browser will then ask for confirmation. If you block the permission, you will have to enable it manually in your settings.
